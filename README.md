# Dayflow

AI-powered timecard exception detection and resolution.

Dayflow identifies time and attendance issues such as missed punches, overtime risks, schedule conflicts, and policy violations. It investigates each exception, proposes a correction with an explanation, and validates the proposed change before human approval.

## Core principles

- **Detect:** Surface timecard exceptions early.
- **Explain:** Show the evidence and policy behind each recommendation.
- **Validate:** Recalculate and verify proposed corrections deterministically.
- **Approve:** Keep a human in control of every change.
- **Audit:** Preserve a traceable record of recommendations and approvals.
